Free pass
pyrexia = fever
bacterial = from bacteria
leucopenia = low white cells
thrombocytopenia = low platelets
exanthem = rash
arboviral = a virus from insects (mosquitoes)
The fever was not from bacteria. Her white cells and platelets were LOW, and she had a rash. That fits a virus carried by mosquitoes (dengue).
Because dengue is a virus. Antibiotics work on bacteria, not on viruses.
